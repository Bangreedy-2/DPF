Walk into every IEP meeting prepared.

IEP paperwork piles up, and the important parts get lost. This binder keeps everything in six tabs, and each tab helps you do something, not only file it.

Why parents choose this binder

  • See if services are actually happening. Log each speech, OT or other session. The tracker compares it with the minutes in the IEP every month and flags anything short.
  • Follow every goal between meetings. One page per goal with a progress graph, and a Goals tab that flags a goal that has stalled.
  • Know what to say when the meeting gets rushed. A one-page script, 8 calm phrases, a 2-week countdown and 40 questions by topic.
  • Put every request in writing. 10 polite, ready-to-send letters and emails.

What's inside

42-page printable binder (US Letter + A4)

  • My Child: all-about-me snapshot, key contacts, key dates, evaluation summaries, "first 30 minutes" checklist
  • The Plan: IEP at a glance, services promised, accommodations check
  • Services: monthly service log, missed sessions log
  • Progress: measurable-goal checker, goal pages with a filled example, progress report checklist
  • Meetings: countdown, top 3 concerns, question bank, meeting script, decisions page
  • Letters & Log: 10 letter templates, communication log, phone call notes
  • Plain-English glossary + 6 printable tab dividers

Google Sheets + Excel tracker (10 tabs)
Dashboard · Services · Service Log · Service Summary · Dates · Goals · Contacts · Communication Log · Decisions · Letters.

FAQ

Do I need Google Sheets?
No. You also get an Excel file, and every page prints.

Will it work for my state?
It works for any US IEP. It's an organizing tool, so it doesn't depend on state rules. Terms and timelines vary by state, so check your state's parent rights booklet for those.

Is this legal advice?
No. For legal questions, contact your state's Parent Training and Information Center, an advocate, or a special education attorney.
