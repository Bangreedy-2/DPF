FAQ

Do I need Google Sheets?
No. You also get an Excel file, and every page prints.

Will it work for my state?
It works for any US IEP. It's an organizing tool, so it doesn't depend on state rules. Terms and timelines vary by state, so check your state's parent rights booklet for those.

Is this legal advice?
No. For legal questions, contact your state's Parent Training and Information Center, an advocate, or a special education attorney.
